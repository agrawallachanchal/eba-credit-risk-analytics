Stress test: headroom to each bank's CET1 requirement

An NPE shock from the ECB 2025 stress test is applied to each bank's gross loans. New NPE are provisioned at the bank's current coverage and charged against June 2025 CET1, with risk-weighted assets held constant. Headroom is the stressed CET1 ratio minus the bank's own published CET1 requirement (the level below which distribution restrictions apply).

Shocks read from ECB Chart 3.7 (2025 stress test final results, p. 27): 1-year baseline +0.8 pp, 1-year adverse +1.2 pp, 3-year baseline +2.4 pp, 3-year adverse +4.1 pp (5.8% at end-2027 from 1.7%, p. 26). With earnings: annualised H1 2025 profit before tax over the horizon, 30% tax on positive results, 30% payout in profit years and none in loss years (EBA 2025 methodological note). Revenues are not stressed, so that view is optimistic. At the default settings the figures equal the Excel model.
